### Mehmet Tuğsuz

M.S. Economics (STEM) at Arizona State University, December 2026. Previously M.A. and B.A.
in Economics at Bilkent. I work on applied econometrics: panel and time-series methods,
causal inference, and empirical asset pricing.

**Current work**

- **[alpha-decay](https://github.com/tugsuz/alpha-decay)** — how fast a published equity
  anomaly stops working once it is public. Across 189 signals in the Chen–Zimmermann
  universe (1926–2024), long-short returns fall 45% after publication; the median signal
  loses 53%. Panel regression with signal fixed effects and date-clustered standard errors,
  cluster-robust covariance written from scratch. Next: a state-space model for the decay
  rate, half-life and long-run floor of each signal. Python.
- **[ECN726TermProject-Replication](https://github.com/tugsuz/ECN726TermProject-Replication)**
  — replication of an applied econometrics paper. Stata.

**Background**

Master's thesis on energy import concentration and welfare: Herfindahl–Hirschman indices
built from a Bloomberg Terminal and regulator sources, supplier-allocation models under
disruption risk, and a Baqaee–Farhi DSGE counterfactual for a 2022 supply cut-off.
Credited for research assistance on a bargaining experiment published in *Experimental
Economics* (2025), where I produced the adjudicated coding of 216 subjects' free-text
messages and ran the regressions behind the paper's content analysis.

Python · SQL · R · Stata · MATLAB
